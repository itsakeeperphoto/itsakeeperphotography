# Surface brief — `/pasco-wa-photographer/`

## Approval

- Direction approved by the user on 2026-08-09: **A+C**.
- A controls the shared EditorialHero, torn-paper transition and arched intro.
- C controls the dark services ledger and the restrained archive rhythm.
- `pasco-approved-manifest.json` is the canonical visual manifest.
- Mock text is never content authority; `content/pages/pasco.json` is binding.

## Visual contract

- Preserve the current It's A Keeper Photography palette, type system, header and shared EditorialHero geometry.
- Hero: real Pasco extended-family background, two decorative real-photo prints, centered two-line H1, no script phrase, scroll button to `#pasco-final`, one paper edge.
- Intro: warm ivory, one arched family portrait, one vertical hairline and copy-led right column.
- Rivers: deep olive-walnut, text left, two photographs right, crossing hairlines suggest confluence without a map or exact meeting point.
- Farmland: wide horizon strip, concise copy and two aligned portrait crops; no floating-card treatment.
- Services: five ruled rows on olive-walnut with one dominant photograph and a restrained arched overlap.
- Gallery: exactly ten verified Pasco photographs in a disciplined editorial grid. Generated mock people never ship.
- Seasons: text-led calendar planning with one wide and two supporting photographs.
- FAQ: four native `details` rows on olive-walnut, one open by default, keyboard-visible focus.
- Final: full-bleed verified Pasco photograph with one square ivory copy panel.

## Prohibited devices

- No copied reference brand, wedding copy, social chrome, tape, splatters or signatures.
- No gradients, glass, rounded cards, drop shadows, generic icon sets, maps, location pins, fake reviews, prices, awards or unverified landmarks.
- No generated photography in production.

## SEO and semantic contract

- Exact H1: `Pasco, WA Photographer`.
- Exact eight H2 headings from the approved content, including the final CTA.
- Exactly eight anchors inside `main`; the hero CTA is a button, not an anchor.
- Five service links, two contextual links and one final contact link.
- Four visible FAQs must match one four-question `FAQPage` graph exactly.
- One Pasco `Service`, one city `spatialCoverage` and one breadcrumb graph.
- Pasco is a service area served from Richland: no Pasco address, coordinates, reviews or aggregate rating.
- Release state: `ready/index`, sitemap and `llms.txt` included.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

- Verify 1440, 1200, 900 and 390 px with no real horizontal overflow.
- At 390 px, protect `Photographer`, the intro H2, `Professional Headshots` and long FAQ labels.
- Dissolve overlaps below 768 px; preserve DOM reading order and meaningful crops.
- Targets are at least 44 px, focus is visible, `prefers-reduced-motion` removes nonessential movement and the hero scroll button transfers focus to `#pasco-final`.
